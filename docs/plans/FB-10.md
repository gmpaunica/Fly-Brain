### Tiny calculation

A rate of 30 Hz with training mean 20 Hz and std 10 Hz becomes `(30-20)/10=1`.
For one hidden unit with weight 1 and bias 0, `tanh(1)=0.761594`. A turn-output
weight of 0.5 gives `tanh(0.5*0.761594)=0.363399`. Actual batches have shape
`[1008,8]`; PyTorch stores the first matrix as `[16,8]` (destination, source),
then computes `X @ W.T + b`. The second matrix is `[2,16]`.

Forward simulation calculates rates and commands. Autograd differentiates the
offline readout loss; Adam changes its 178 weights/biases. It never differentiates
the BANC spikes or alters the source graph. A lower training error or passing
software suite alone is not evidence of learned navigation.
