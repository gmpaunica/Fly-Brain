## Exact model assumptions

Time is seconds; voltage/current/weights use arbitrary normalized model units,
not mV or amperes. Default dt=0.001, tau=0.020, rest=reset=0, threshold=1.
For constant external current I during a substep, let a=exp(-dt/tau):

```text
candidate = rest + (voltage - rest)*a + I*(1-a) + W @ previous_spikes
new_spikes = candidate >= threshold
voltage = where(new_spikes, reset, candidate)
```

I is the steady-state voltage displacement from rest. Each W[post,pre] is
an instantaneous voltage jump from a spike in the previous step, applied at
the end of this step. This combines exact passive integration with a discrete
delayed synaptic jump; it is an assumed model. No refractory counter or delay
beyond one step; at most one spike/neuron/substep, discarded overshoot, and
continuous strong drive can fire on consecutive steps. Negative currents and
weights are allowed in the generic population. No surrogate gradient/training.

Tiny fixture: tau=dt/log(2), so a=0.5. A receives I=2 and fires at equality
on step 1; W[B,A]=1 causes B to fire on step 2 with zero new input. With
W[A,B]=0 no reverse propagation occurs. Reset removes both pending spikes
and voltages, restores rest, and resets step counter.

Graph: sensory_left 10, sensory_right 10, interneuron_left 25,
interneuron_right 25, motor_left 10, motor_forward 10, motor_right 10.
Full same-side sensory→interneuron and interneuron→motor blocks only;
all other edges zero. Positive jumps uniform [0.096,0.144), locally seeded.
Tonic forward current=1.3; no other tonic drive. Same-side wiring, gains and
tonic movement are hand-written scaffolding, with no navigation claim.

Encoder: I_left/right = 2*min(concentration/0.025,1), separately applied
to the corresponding sensory population. Zero concentrations give zero
sensory current. Scale is arbitrary, explicit, and unrelated to source
coordinates. Encoding adds no steering answer. Float32 default, float64
supported; CPU only, bool spikes, int64 counts, exact shapes checked.
Network advance defaults to 20 substeps (0.020 s); raw generic population step
uses configured dt. Snapshot tensors are copies and cannot advance state.
